I lead the SeaQL Lab at Virginia Tech, an interdisciplinary hub in quantitative and computational ecology, employing cutting-edge methods in statistics and data science to conduct innovative research on major global change ecology, fisheries science, and conservation. “SeaQL” stands for a turning point in marine conservation and sustainability and highlights my group's commitment to writing a bright sequel in the history of ocean use by embracing the big data revolution and advances in analytics and ocean technology.

Please visit [http://SeaQL.org](http://SeaQL.org)
