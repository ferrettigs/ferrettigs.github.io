I recently had the pleasure of speaking with students from around the world about sharks, marine conservation, and what it means to pursue a career in science.

We discussed everything from the remarkable abilities of sharks to the challenges of reconstructing historical ecosystems, working with incomplete data, and translating scientific evidence into conservation action.
